# Inquiry Web

**▶ View it live: https://cvd9683-gif.github.io/inquiry-web/**

Cathy Doss · NYU thesis: hunches, questions, and sources.

> **How do people relate to AI, and how is that changing the way we trust, learn, and create?**

An interactive map of my thesis inquiry. It combines two progress updates:

- **Progress Update 1:** my inquiry space map. Click any word to zoom into the hunches it fed.
- **Progress Update 2:** research findings and a refined inquiry space. Open the **Progress Update 2** button in the header.
- **Progress Update 3:** a bank of 8 creative exploration ideas across all five hunches, each with a concept sketch (none are made yet; I'll pick 2–5). Open **Progress Update 3** in the header (`#/x`); each idea has its own page (e.g. `#/x/x1`), also linked from the ✦ chip on its question's page.

## Exploring it

- **Map → word → hunch → question → source:** each click zooms one level deeper.
- **Back**, **Esc**, or the browser's back button go up a level. The breadcrumbs at the top jump to any level.
- Every view has its own link (for example `#/h1/q1`), so you can share a specific question or source.
- On a phone, it switches to a stacked list.

## Editing

Everything is one file, `index.html`. All the text (hunches, questions, sources, findings, experts, explorations, and the map's words) lives in the `DATA` block at the top. Photos and videos for explorations go in an `explorations/` folder and are listed in each exploration's `media`. Edit it, commit, and push; the live site updates within a minute or two.
